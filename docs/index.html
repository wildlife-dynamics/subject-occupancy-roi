Subject Occupancy in Regions

Estimates each subject's home range from its EarthRanger GPS tracks, then measures how much of each region (a conservancy, park or census block, for example) that home range covers.

What it produces

OutputDescription
Home Range MapEach subject's elliptical time-density home range, coloured by percentile (50% to 99.9%), over the chosen regions in their EarthRanger colours.
Occupancy tableFor each subject and each region its home range reaches: the region's area, the area of it the home range covers, and that as a percentage of the region.
CSV filesThe occupancy table, one file per dashboard view.

Occupancy in one line

Occupancy (%) = area of the region inside the subject's 99.9% home range ÷ the region's area × 100. A subject whose home range covers a quarter of a park has 25% occupancy of that park.

Requirements

  • An EarthRanger connection set up in the workflow runner.
  • A subject group whose subjects have GPS fixes in the time range.
  • Regions saved in EarthRanger as polygon features, in a feature set (e.g. Boundaries) or with a feature type (e.g. Conservancy).
